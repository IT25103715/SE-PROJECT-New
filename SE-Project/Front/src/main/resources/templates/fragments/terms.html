Terms & Conditions and Cancellation Policy

Sample terms for demonstration purposes only.

1. Bookings

A booking is confirmed once payment is completed and a ticket with a QR code is issued. Please check the movie, showtime, hall and seats before paying - each ticket is valid only for the showtime and seats shown on it.

2. Payment

Prices are shown in Sri Lankan Rupees and include any coupon discount applied at checkout. Bank transfer bookings stay pending until the uploaded receipt has been approved.

3. Cancellation Policy

Bookings can be cancelled from "My Bookings". A booking cancelled before the showtime starts is refunded in full; a booking cancelled after the showtime has started is not refunded. Cancelling a booking voids its ticket, so it can no longer be used for entry.

4. Entry

Please arrive before the showtime and have your QR ticket ready. Each ticket can be scanned only once. The cinema may refuse entry for tickets that have already been used.

5. Changes

The cinema may change showtimes or halls where necessary and will contact affected customers. These terms may be updated from time to time.
